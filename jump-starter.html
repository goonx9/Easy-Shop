Step-by-Step

How To Jump Start in 3 Steps

Follow these 3 simple steps to get your vehicle running within seconds.

01

Connect Clamps

Attach the RED clamp to the battery's positive (+) post and the BLACK clamp to the negative (-) terminal.

02

Start the Engine

Switch on the jump starter power unit and turn your vehicle ignition to start the engine smoothly.

03

Disconnect Clamps

Once the car is idling smoothly, remove the clamps from the battery and store the jump starter back in its case.
